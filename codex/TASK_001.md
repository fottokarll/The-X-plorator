## B. Visual prototype
- One small navigable 3D star sector and code-generated unique compact spacecraft, soft engine emissive glow.
- Satisfying pitch/yaw/roll, strafing and forward thrust; optional auto-stabilization, boost, forgiving damping, smooth chase camera.
- Three distinct procedural gradient planets: oceanic teal, volcanic coral and frozen blue; original shader-created bands/rings and soft atmosphere transition.
- Sparse instanced stars and low-poly asteroid cluster, one scannable object as a visual interaction only.
- Seven connected hex modules, isometric camera toggled from flight. Placement ghost as non-economic UX test.
- Readable mobile/desktop controls and HUD; quality toggle and mute/audio opt-in.

## C. Acceptance / delivery
- Local launch instructions; browser boots without console errors; flight and both cameras demonstrably work.
- All three planets visibly different; style matches ART_BIBLE and user's reference *direction* without publishing moodboard artwork.
- Keyboard/mouse and narrow 360px mobile layout tested; measured FPS if possible; screenshots from actual running app (not generated mockups).
- Exact changed files, commands and test output in PR; no P0 publish, no payments, no fake saves, stop for owner feedback.
