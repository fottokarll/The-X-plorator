# Art Bible v0.1

## Artistic identity
**Ethereal · illustrated · geometric · restrained.** Avoid photorealism, busy neon, hard pixel-art and generic placeholder grey. Original inspiration was user's private moodboard — use for visual direction only, NEVER ship or redistribute the reference images.

## Primary palette
- Deep space `#090E22` / Midnight `#16283D`
- Teal `#31C7C4` / Cyan `#87D6DA`
- Rose `#E65C93` / Coral `#F08A75`
- Gold `#F8C88D` / Bone typography `#EBEFF5`

## Materials and shapes
Faceted rocks, geometric spacecraft, shader-created gradients, painterly planet bands, elegant translucent ring geometry, soft bloom and minimal distant star particles. Generate meshes/shaders programmatically wherever possible, with no PNG/GLB dependence in prototype. Typography and HUD must remain readable on narrow phone layouts.

## Planetary visual poetry
For each planet define `VisualAtmosphereProfile`: dominant palette, sun tint, distance haze, ring colour, glow intensity, decorative noise and sound motif. Blend atmosphere over approach distance instead of abrupt full-screen colour switching. Visual identity should remain distinctive even at mobile quality settings.

## Sound design
Quiet original/licensed piano notes, low pads and negative space. Start audio only from a user gesture. Mute and volume controls. Do not generate expensive/unlicensed assets or embed copyrighted tracks.

## Two cameras
Flight: forgiving chase perspective, clear silhouette and forward view. Build: rotatable isometric 3D focused on flat hex base. Galaxy map: sparse legible graphics and mission nodes.

## Visual acceptance
Three distinguishable planets; a beautiful but readable orbital approach; a unique low-poly player ship; meaningful hex structure; responsive 360–390px UI; reduced-motion option; report captured screenshots and *measured* FPS.
