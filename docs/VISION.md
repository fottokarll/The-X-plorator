# Vision — The X-plorator

Earth has become nearly uninhabitable after resource depletion, agricultural collapse, violent weather and nuclear catastrophe. An original, fictional visionary's space infrastructure allows a handful of evacuation ships to escape. Each player captains a ship carrying scarce supplies and a small group of survivors.

**Player fantasy:** solitary explorer → builder → colony organiser → architect of humanity's rebirth.

**Emotional identity:** tranquil exploratory wonder rather than domination. Planets are living colour poems with subtle atmospheres and calm original/licensed piano ambience.

**North star — Genesis Initiative:** find a viable homeworld, sustain a growing population, assemble functioning colonies, connect renewable energy/food/transport, and activate Genesis. Open-ended exploration remains after completion.

**Release target:** a playable Project 0 City experience on a NEW lot, never STARBOX #696. Public P0 access and account policies must be respected; local family playtesting remains separate.
